## Quick Start Checklist

1. Set the default commission and cookie lifetime in [Configuration](./configuration.md).
2. Write your program terms in **Affiliate Program Rules & Terms**.
3. Upload at least one promotional banner.
4. Choose the payout methods you support, and decide whether to keep **Auto Approve Commissions** on (default) or approve commissions manually.
5. Create a test affiliate, open their link in a private window and place an order as a guest or another customer (affiliates never earn on their own orders).
6. Check that a **Pending** commission appears in **Affiliate → Commissions**, then complete the order and check that it becomes **Approved** (or approve it yourself if auto-approval is off).
